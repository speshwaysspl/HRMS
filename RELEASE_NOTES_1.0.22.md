## Full release notes

### Everyone
- **Digital ID card**: front and back Speshway ID card with your photo,
  employee ID and a scan-to-save-contact QR code. View it from My Profile and
  download / share it as a PDF.
- **Sign in**: new sign-in screen with a live clock and greeting; the page
  scrolls when the keyboard opens.
- **Welcome animation**: new launch animation while your session loads.
- **Side menu**: the page now slides back to reveal the menu, with your photo,
  name and role on top. Opening or closing it no longer reloads the screen.
- **Notifications**: new in-app notification pop-up while the app is open.
- **Pull to refresh**: a balloon inflates as you pull and pops into confetti;
  now works on every screen, even short ones.
- **Announcements**: themed cards per type (important, quote, festival, event,
  achievement, notice, birthday, anniversary, holiday), pinned posts at the
  top, "New" on posts you haven't opened; tap an image to zoom.
- **Images**: announcement images load faster and are kept on the phone.
- **Sign out** now removes all of the account's data, notifications and App
  Lock setting from the phone.

### Admin / HR
- Add or change an employee's ID-card photo (optional) when adding or editing
  them; position or remove it from the ID card.
- Announcements: choose the type, pin to top, set a "show until" date, and see
  who has seen each announcement.

### Fixes
- Screens no longer reload every second.
- Pull to refresh works on My Teams, Attendance, Leaves, Calendar, Tasks,
  Announcements, Feedback and Payslips.
- Notification pop-ups show reliably while the app is open.

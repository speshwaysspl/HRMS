# Speshway HRMS — Version 1.0.21 (build 24)

Release date: 29 October 2026

## Play Store "What's new" (under 500 characters)

```
<en-US>
• Redesigned team screens: milestones, attendance and members
• Search and filter team members; see each member's task progress
• Milestones show done, open and overdue tasks
• Attendance report totals now match the calendar

• Fewer duplicate notifications
```

## Full release notes

### Team leads
- Team screen header shows the team lead; tabs have icons.
- Milestones: Open / Closed summary cards, cards with Done / Open / Overdue
  counts and a status chip, and an illustrated empty state.
- Attendance: date and Excel card, Present / Half Day / Absent / Not Marked
  counts, member search, and P / Half / A per member.
- Members: search, filter (overdue / pending / all done), team totals and a
  progress card per member.
- My Teams: search, summary card, team badges and lead names.

### Employees
- Attendance report cards match the calendar (holidays and weekends are no
  longer counted as absences).
- A day with a check-in but no check-out is marked Present once the day ends.
- Payslip cards show total deductions (PF, professional tax, loss of pay and
  other deductions).
- Home screen shows your designation and department from your profile.

### Notifications and sign-in
- Each notification appears once, even if the phone has an old push token.
- You stay signed in until you log out.

### Other
- Recent root logins and the admin account-deletion request were removed from
  Settings.
